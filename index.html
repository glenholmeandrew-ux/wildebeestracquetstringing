About Wildebeest Racquet Stringing

We're a specialist racquet stringing service in Preston, Lancashire, serving both squash and tennis players for over 8 years. Our passion for the game shows in every string we tension.

As a keen squash competitor myself, I understand what players need: precision, reliability, and a personal touch. We deliver all three. We specialise in matching your preferred string type and tension to your playing style and racquet specifications.

Why choose us? Fast turnaround (24 hours), competitive pricing, a wide range of string brands, and genuine expertise in both sports. Whether you're a casual player or competing at a high level, we've got you covered.
